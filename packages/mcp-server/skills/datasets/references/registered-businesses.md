  how many businesses operate or have operated in SF, in which neighborhood or corridor,
  under what NAICS/LIC code, or whether a business is still active.

# registered-businesses

SF's open-data registered-business-locations export, converted from CSV — by far the
largest dataset here. `Ownership Name`/`DBA Name`/address fields are free text with no
deduplication, so the same real-world business can appear multiple times under slightly
different spellings; `UniqueID`/`Location Id` identify registrations, not deduplicated
businesses. A handful of `Business Location` points are geocoding outliers well outside
San Francisco. Load it with `load_available_dataset({ name: "registered-businesses" })`.

## What it can answer

Business counts by `Neighborhoods - Analysis Boundaries`, `Business Corridor`, or
`Supervisor District`; by `Self-Reported NAICS Code` or `LIC Code Description`; open vs.
closed (a null `Business End Date` means still active); and longevity (`Business Start
Date` to `Business End Date`). There is no revenue, employee-count, or business-outcome
data, and with no deduplication, "how many businesses" answers are registration counts,
not a guaranteed count of distinct real-world businesses.
