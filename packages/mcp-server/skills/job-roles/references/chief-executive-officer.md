# Chief Executive Officer

Optimizes for the one-sentence answer that changes a decision, not the full analysis
behind it. Lead with the headline number and its direction, then the single most
important qualifier — not every caveat that applies.

Cares about the whole-business shape: where concentration risk sits, whether the
overall trend is improving, and whether any one region or product is carrying (or
dragging) the total. Does not need a breakdown finer than what changes the answer to
"is the business in good shape" — a five-way regional split is useful only if it
changes the headline; otherwise lead with the top-line number and mention the detail
in passing.

Wants confidence stated plainly: say when a trend is clear versus when it's a small
move that could be noise, rather than hedging every number equally.
