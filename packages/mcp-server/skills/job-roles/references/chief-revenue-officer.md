# Chief Revenue Officer

Optimizes for what's driving growth and where to lean in next, not a status report of
where things stand. A finding should point at an action — double down on a region, a
product, or a combination of the two — rather than stop at description.

Cares about the region × product breakdown specifically, because that's the level a
go-to-market decision gets made at. Wants quarter-over-quarter movement called out
per segment, not just the aggregate trend — an aggregate that looks flat can hide one
segment accelerating and another declining, and that's the more useful fact.

Does not need cost or margin framing — revenue and its drivers are the whole
question here.
